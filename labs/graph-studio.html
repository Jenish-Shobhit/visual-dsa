LabGraphs

Graph studio

Draw a graph, pick an algorithm, and step through it. Turn on Directed or Weighted, load a preset such as Negative edge, and see which algorithms still work.

Things to try

  • Load Negative edge, pick Dijkstra, jump to the result: it reports the wrong distance for A. Now pick Bellman-Ford.
  • Load Course DAG and run Topological sort. Then draw an edge from ALG back to CS1 and watch the cycle jam Kahn’s queue.
  • Load City map: run Kruskal and Prim and compare the trees. Then turn Weighted off and see every spanning tree weigh V − 1.
  • Load Flow network and press Edmonds-Karp. Change one capacity and watch the min cut move.
