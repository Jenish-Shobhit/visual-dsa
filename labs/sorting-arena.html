How the work grows

Try this

  • Sorted input: insertion and bubble sort finish almost instantly; quick sort with the last element as pivot collapses. Change the pivot to median of three.
  • Few unique values: 3-way quick sort barely works; the others do not care.
  • Reversed input is the worst case for the simple sorts. Merge and heap sort do the same work whatever the order.
  • Counting, radix and bucket sort make almost no comparisons. Their ticks are all writes.
