LabTrees and heaps

Tree studio

Pick a structure, type a value, and watch every comparison, rotation and swap. Insert ten random keys, then 1 to 15 in order, and see why balanced trees exist.

Things to try

  • Turn on Compare, then press Insert 1 to 15 in order. The BST becomes a stick 14 links tall; the AVL and red-black trees stay near 3 and 5.
  • On the AVL tree insert 30, 20, 10: a straight line, one rotation. Then 30, 10, 20: a bend, two rotations.
  • In a min-heap insert 9, 8, 7, 6, 5, then extract three times and watch the last leaf travel to the root and sift down.
  • In the trie insert tea, ten and to: the shared prefix is stored once.
